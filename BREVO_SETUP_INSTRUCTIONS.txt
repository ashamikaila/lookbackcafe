  BREVO (SENDINBLUE) SETUP - QUICK START GUIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ WHY BREVO?
- FREE: 300 emails per day
- NO APP PASSWORDS needed
- Reliable email delivery
- Easy setup (5 minutes)
- Real emails to real addresses

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: CREATE BREVO ACCOUNT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to: https://www.brevo.com/
2. Click "Sign up free"
3. Fill in your details:
   - Email
   - Password
   - Company name (can be "Look Back Café")
4. Verify your email address

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: GET SMTP CREDENTIALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Log in to Brevo dashboard
2. Click your name (top right) > Settings
3. Click "SMTP & API" in left menu
4. Click "SMTP" tab
5. You'll see:
   
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ SMTP Server: smtp-relay.brevo.com       │
   │ Port: 587                               │
   │ Login: your-email@example.com           │
   │ SMTP Key: [Generate new SMTP key]      │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────┘

6. Click "Generate a new SMTP key"
7. Copy the key (you won't see it again!)
8. SAVE THESE CREDENTIALS

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: VERIFY SENDER EMAIL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

⚠️ IMPORTANT: You can only send from verified emails!

1. In Brevo, go to: Settings > Senders & IP
2. Click "Add a sender"
3. Enter your email (e.g., noreply@lookbackcafe.com or your Gmail)
4. Click "Add"
5. Check your email inbox
6. Click the verification link
7. Wait for "Verified" status

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4: UPDATE YOUR CODE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Open: lookbackcafe/website/config/email.php

Find these lines and update:

define('SMTP_HOST', 'smtp-relay.brevo.com');
define('SMTP_PORT', 587);
define('SMTP_USERNAME', 'YOUR_BREVO_EMAIL');      ← Your Brevo login email
define('SMTP_PASSWORD', 'YOUR_SMTP_KEY');          ← The SMTP key you copied
define('SMTP_FROM_EMAIL', 'YOUR_VERIFIED_EMAIL');  ← Email you verified in Step 3

Example:
define('SMTP_USERNAME', 'john@example.com');
define('SMTP_PASSWORD', 'xsmtpsib-a1b2c3d4...');
define('SMTP_FROM_EMAIL', 'noreply@lookbackcafe.com');

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5: TEST IT!
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open: http://localhost:8080/website/test_newsletter.php
2. Enter YOUR email address
3. Click "Send Test Newsletter"
4. Check your inbox (and spam folder)
5. You should receive the email!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ "SMTP Error: Could not authenticate"
   → Check SMTP_USERNAME and SMTP_PASSWORD are correct
   → Use SMTP key, NOT your Brevo account password

❌ "SMTP Error: Could not connect"
   → Check internet connection
   → Verify SMTP_HOST is 'smtp-relay.brevo.com'
   → Verify SMTP_PORT is 587

❌ No email received
   → Check spam/junk folder
   → Verify sender email is verified in Brevo
   → Check Brevo dashboard > Logs for errors

❌ "Sender not verified"
   → Go to Brevo > Settings > Senders & IP
   → Click verification link in email
   → Wait for "Verified" status
